About Me

Namaste! Mera naam Obito hai. Main ek passionate self-taught developer hoon jo technology aur coding ki duniya mein naya-naya kadam rakh raha hai.

Maine apni coding journey ki shuruat ek smartphone aur Termux (Linux terminal for Android) se ki hai. Bina kisi laptop ke, sirf apne phone par commands chalakar, scripts likhkar aur networks explore karke maine yahan tak ka safar tay kiya hai.

Maine ye website isliye banayi hai taaki main jo bhi naye tools aur projects seekhta hoon, unhe aam logon ke saath share kar sakun. Mere liye coding sirf ek skill nahi, balki problems solve karne ka ek mazedaar tarika hai.

Yahan jo bhi tools hain, wo sab 100% free hain aur main aage bhi aise naye tools add karta rahunga. Agar aapko meri mehnat pasand aaye, toh apne doston ke saath is website ko share karein!
